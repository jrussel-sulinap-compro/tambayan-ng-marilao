JWU Coffee Bar

Located at 2nd floor of the building that gives a great view while enjoying your coffee. It is the perfect spot if you are looking to unwind, catch up on some work, or enjoy a quiet afternoon with friends.

What makes this place special is the relaxing atmosphere combined with large open windows that let in plenty of natural light. Sitting by the window gives you a peaceful elevated perspective of the surrounding area while sipping on your favorite cold brew or warm espresso.
