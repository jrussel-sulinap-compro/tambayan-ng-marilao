About Me

Hello! I'm John Russel Sulinap, a passionate coffee enthusiast and community member of Marilao. I love sharing the rich culture and flavors of our local coffee scene.

Whenever I have the chance, I like to visit different coffee shops in Marilao and try their unique blends and specialties.

I also check the appearance and ambiance of each location to ensure a great experience for fellow coffee lovers.

With my experience, I aim to provide insights and recommendations for those looking to explore the vibrant coffee culture in Marilao.
